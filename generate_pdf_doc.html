1. Executive Summary & Product Vision

AeroPulse Diagnostics is an advanced, privacy-first single-page web application (SPA) that enables users to diagnose mechanical and electrical household appliance malfunctions purely through sound. Using either a live microphone stream or an uploaded audio file, the system’s local algorithmic engine decomposes frequency harmonics, isolates anomalous acoustic signatures, and matches them against curated failure models to return high-fidelity DIY repair instructions.

Zero-Guesswork Acoustic Classification: Unlike legacy diagnostic checklists where non-technical users must diagnose whether an appliance is making a "grinding", "humming", or "rattling" sound, AeroPulse removes manual sound selection entirely. The user selects only the appliance type and manufacturer brand; the engine’s automated spectral analysis isolates the defect signature directly from the audio recording.

Privacy-First Architecture

Audio processing, waveform visualization, and diagnostic inference are evaluated entirely client-side in the browser. No raw audio blobs or audio recordings leave the user’s local workstation.

iQOO Smartphone Aesthetics

Built on a modern utility theme: Cyber Yellow (#FFD100), Deep Obsidian (#0F0F11), Crisp White, and Clean Slate (#E5E7EB). Avoids generic gradients and AI slop patterns.

2. Architectural Overview & Workflow

The application is structured as a 4-step state machine orchestrating data capture, audio processing, algorithmic classification, and technical report delivery.

1
Step 1: Appliance Identification (ApplianceForm.js)

The user selects the appliance category (Washing Machine, Refrigerator, Split AC, Microwave, Dishwasher, Dryer), selects the specific model type (e.g., Front Load, Top Load, Inverter), and specifies the manufacturer brand (Samsung, LG, Whirlpool, Bosch, Haier, IFB, Godrej, Panasonic, etc.). Manual sound picking is omitted.

2
Step 2: Acoustic Signal Capture (AudioRecorder.js & WaveformVisual.js)

Dual-mode audio capture interface: (1) Live microphone capture via navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia with an automated 10-second cutoff and circular countdown timer; (2) Drag-and-drop or browse file upload supporting .wav, .mp3, .m4a, .webm, .ogg, and .aac. Features an integrated HTML5 Canvas oscilloscope with Web Audio API AnalyserNode.

3
Step 3: Automated Spectral Analysis Pipeline (audioEngine.js)

Executes a 2.8-second simulated machine inference cycle with spinning rings and progressive pipeline logs. Decompresses bitstreams, computes FFT spectral density, and classifies the acoustic signature without user input.

4
Step 4: Diagnostic Report Dashboard (DiagnosticReport.js)

Renders a high-contrast dashboard detailing the auto-detected sound, dominant frequency, spectral pattern, signal-to-noise ratio, technical diagnosis, safety warnings, numbered DIY steps, required replacement parts, and cost estimates.

5. Component-by-Component Technical Breakdown

ApplianceForm.js — Category & Brand Selector

Renders 6 interactive category tiles (🫧 Washing Machine, ❄️ Refrigerator, 🌬️ Split AC, 📡 Microwave, 🍽️ Dishwasher, 💨 Dryer). Dynamically cascades appliance types and selects from 15 global manufacturers (Samsung, LG, Haier, Bosch, Whirlpool, IFB, Godrej, Panasonic, Voltas, Daikin, Carrier, Blue Star, Electrolux, Siemens, Hitachi).

AudioRecorder.js & WaveformVisual.js — Audio Capture & Canvas Engine

Manages the browser microphone stream via navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia. Enforces a 10-second countdown with an SVG circular progress indicator. The WaveformVisual component uses window.requestAnimationFrame and an AnalyserNode (FFT size 2048, smoothing 0.75) to render a crisp glowing yellow oscilloscope on a black backdrop, scaled by devicePixelRatio for Retina clarity.

DiagnosticReport.js — High-Contrast Reporting Dashboard

Features a color-coded gradient severity bar (#EF4444 High Risk, #F97316 Moderate, #22C55E Low Risk), an AUTO-DIAGNOSED badge, a confidence progress meter, safety hazard callouts, detailed technical explanations, step-by-step DIY repair guides, and a print stylesheet for paper or PDF export.

6. Safety Hazards & DIY Protocols

Every diagnosis embeds non-negotiable safety rules appropriate to the target appliance:

☠️ LETHAL HIGH VOLTAGE (Microwave Magnetron & Capacitors):
High-voltage circuits store lethal charge (up to 6,000V / 1µF) even when unplugged. Always bridge terminals with a 20kΩ/10W resistor probe before touching internal components.
⚡ MAINS ISOLATION & CAPACITOR DISCHARGE (ACs & Washers):
Cut power at the main circuit breaker rather than relying on remote controls or wall switches. Motors and condenser fan boards maintain high residual charge.
🧊 REFRIGERANT SAFETY & EVAPORATOR FIN HAZARDS:
Refrigerant circuits require certified technicians with brazing and recovery equipment. Aluminum evaporator coil fins are razor-sharp; always wear protective gloves.
